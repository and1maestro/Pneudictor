Pneudictor is an innovative tool that uses advanced neural network and machine learning algorithms to help doctors detect pneumonia.


Our model was trained on thousands of verified X-ray images. It analyzes images pixel by pixel to identify subtle signs of disease. Patient clinical data (temperature, age, and symptoms) are also considered to produce an accurate prediction.

Artificial intelligence

Neural networks analyze X-ray images with high accuracy.

Speed

Instant analysis results save valuable time during diagnosis.

Support for doctors

A second opinion helps reduce the risk of medical errors.

Medical disclaimer

This application is a decision support tool. All results are provided for informational purposes. Artificial intelligence cannot replace a doctor's clinical judgment. The final decision on diagnosis and treatment always rests with a medical professional.
